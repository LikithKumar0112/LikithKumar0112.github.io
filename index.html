05 / ENGINEERING DEEP DIVE

How I solve
real problems.

The interesting part isn't the tool. It's the chain from failure signal to repeatable engineering outcome.

Incident response, redesigned

AI-AUGMENTED OPERATIONS

THE PROBLEM

When a Kubernetes workload fails, engineers traditionally jump between kubectl, pod events, logs, Prometheus queries and CI/CD history. That creates context switching and slows down diagnosis.

THE APPROACH

MCP servers expose Jenkins, Kubernetes state, pod events and Prometheus queries as tools that Claude can reason over. K8sGPT and Gordon add automated diagnosis for common Kubernetes failures.

THE FLOW

Signal → collect context → correlate → explain likely cause → identify the next action → validate the result. The goal is not “AI magic”; it is reducing repetitive operational work.

WHY IT MATTERS

Engineers spend less time manually stitching together operational evidence and more time making the underlying platform reliable. This is the direction I want to explore further in platform engineering.
